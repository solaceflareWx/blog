# 2011 Tornado Outbreak

___
## About
the 2011 tornado outbreak had 368 confirmed tornados in 21 states including Alabama, Gorgia, Kentucky, Louisiana, New York and Virginia. this outbreak followed a high upper level trough moving into the southern plains where an extratropical cyclone (a strong warm and wet low pressure) developed ahead of this trough between Oklahoma and Missouri. 

this elevated the risk for severe thunderstorms including extended long track tornados. CAPE values sat at about 3000-4000 j/kg around Texas. 

the system was predicted to mostly shoot off supercells during the afternoon and evening as it went over a mesoscale convective complex. this means it went over a well organized cluster of thunderstorms that peak at night. The mesoscale convective complex (called the MCC) is a thunderstorm that is long lived ( roughly 12-24 hours ) but has to hold −32 °C (−26 °F), or an area of cloud top that is 50,000 km2 (19,000 sq mi) with temperature less than or equal to −52 °C (−62 °F). Size requirements must be met for 6 hours
